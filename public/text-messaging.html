Text Message Program and Consent

Mow Pro GA is operated by Mow Pro Lawn Care LLC, Douglasville, GA. This page explains how our text messages work.

How you opt in

On our website quote form (mowproga.com, "Request This Quote"), you enter your name, address and mobile phone number. You can also sign up for texts right here. On this page, and on our quote form, there is an unchecked checkbox that you tick to agree to texts. On the quote form it is optional: you can request a quote without ticking it, and we will not text you unless you do.

You can also start a conversation by calling or texting our business number. We reply only to people who contacted us. Customers who gave us their number for service are texted only about their service and account. Consent to texting is not a condition of buying any service.

What we send

  • Replies to quote requests and missed calls, and a link to our online quote form
  • Service scheduling and reminders
  • Invoices and payment reminders
  • Customer service replies

Frequency, cost, opting out

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also email joseph@mowproga.com.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
