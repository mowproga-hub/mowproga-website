Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Mow Pro Lawn Care LLC, doing business as Mow Pro GA ("Mow Pro GA," "we," "us," or "our"), operates mowproga.com (the "Site") and provides lawn care services in and around Douglasville, GA. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote, contact us by phone, text, or chat, or otherwise use the Site, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and property address
  • Details about your yard (size, condition, service preferences) that you provide in the quote form
  • Messages you send through our chat widget or by text
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, collected automatically through analytics tools

How We Use Your Information

  • To prepare and send you a service quote
  • To schedule, perform, and follow up on lawn care service
  • To respond to questions sent through our chat widget, by text, or by phone
  • To improve our Site and service offerings

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (for example, scheduling, payment processing, or SMS/text messaging tools), and only to the extent needed for them to perform those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Text Messages

If you provide your phone number to request a quote or communicate with us, you may receive text messages related to scheduling, quotes, and service updates. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of text communications, or reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with or sold to any third parties. Phone numbers are shared only with the texting provider that delivers our messages, and only to send them.

Cookies and Analytics

Our Site may use basic analytics tools to understand how visitors use the Site (for example, which pages are viewed). This information is used in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you beyond what you've directly provided to us.

Data Retention

We retain customer and quote information for as long as needed to provide service, maintain business records, and comply with legal or tax obligations.

Your Choices

You may ask us to update or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the information below. Note that we may need to retain certain records for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults arranging lawn care for their property. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
