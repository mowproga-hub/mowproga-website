Local questions

Milwaukie small-yard questions

Do you need a full-size mower for a compact lot? +

Not necessarily. Small separated areas or narrow access may be better suited to other equipment.

Can paths between grass sections be left clear? +

Yes. A proper finish includes blowing clippings from the agreed walks and hard surfaces.

What if there are stairs down to the back lawn? +

Tell the operator where the steps are so they can plan a safe route or use a different tool.

Does a dry summer change the visit interval? +

It can, especially if unirrigated cool-season grass is growing slowly.
