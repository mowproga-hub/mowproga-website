Local questions

Juanita lawn-and-leaf questions

Can thick leaves stay on the grass until spring? +

A heavy matted layer can block light and trap moisture, so timely removal may help the lawn.

Does leaf collection come with every mow? +

No. A full seasonal clearing should be described and priced separately.

Can a mower reach grass behind garden stairs? +

Access must be assessed; another route or different equipment may be needed.

How should roots be finished? +

Keep mower wheels and cutting blades clear of exposed roots and use careful border trimming.
