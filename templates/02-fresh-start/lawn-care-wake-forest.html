Local questions

What Wake Forest homeowners ask

Are autumn leaves removed with every mowing visit? +

Not automatically. Heavy leaf gathering is a separate task that should be agreed on before the job.

What happens if grass keeps growing under falling leaves? +

The operator may need to coordinate mowing with cleanup to avoid leaving debris matted against the turf.

Can mowing work around exposed tree roots? +

Careful trimming is often more appropriate at the root edge than pushing heavy equipment over it.

What should I send for a sloping backyard? +

A photograph that shows the grade and the accessible gate is more helpful than a top-down yard-size estimate.
