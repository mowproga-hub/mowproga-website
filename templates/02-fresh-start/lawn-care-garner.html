Garner drainage and mowing questions

Can a soggy patch be mowed the day after rain? +

Sometimes not. Ruts and soil compaction can be more damaging than an extra day of grass growth.

Should I include my drainage dip in yard square footage? +

Will shade near the back fence affect service? +

It may keep soil damp and turf growing at a different rate from the sunny front yard.

Why mention my long driveway edge? +
