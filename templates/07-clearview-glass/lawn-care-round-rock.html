Local questions

Round Rock turf-and-hardscape questions

Can a trimmer scatter rocks from a landscape bed? +

It can if used carelessly. Stone-bordered areas need a controlled finishing method and adequate clearance.

Should St. Augustinegrass be cut like bermuda? +

No. They have different preferred mowing heights and growth habits.

What if the lawn dries quickly in full sun? +

Heat and moisture stress should be considered before shortening grass or fixing a rigid mowing frequency.

Do sprinkler heads affect the quote? +

Their positions help determine a safe equipment route and any necessary detail trimming.
