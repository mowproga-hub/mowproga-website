Local questions

Oregon City slope questions

Can a standard mower be carried down terrace stairs? +

That is not a safe assumption. Each section needs a suitable access route and equipment choice.

Does wet soil make a hillside harder to maintain? +

Yes. Reduced traction increases risk and may require waiting for a drier day.

Are retaining-wall edges part of ordinary mowing? +

They can involve separate hand trimming; the scope should identify those edges before the visit.

How do you estimate a lawn with several levels? +

Photographs from different heights show actual turf areas and access obstacles that maps cannot reveal.
