Local questions

Orenco turf questions answered

Does new sod need the same cut as established grass? +

Newly established turf may require different timing and equipment care. The operator should check its condition first.

Why might a paver-bordered strip be time-consuming? +

It has a high amount of edging relative to the grass area, so finishing work becomes more significant.

What happens after several rainy days? +

A lawn may be growing well while its soil is still too soft for clean mower passes.

Should I include a photo beneath mature trees? +

Yes. It may reveal thin turf, roots and shade conditions not visible from the street.
