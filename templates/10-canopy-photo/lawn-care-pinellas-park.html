Local questions

Pinellas Park narrow-lawn questions

Can a mower fit through a very narrow side gate? +

That depends on the opening. A measurement helps select safe equipment or a different approach.

Do bahia and St. Augustine need identical cutting heights? +

No. Their preferred mowing heights differ, so the turf should be identified.

How are tight paver edges finished? +

With a controlled trimming pass that avoids damaging the paving or scattering debris.

Does a compact lot always have a lower labor time? +

Not if several separate strips and many obstacles require extensive detail work.
