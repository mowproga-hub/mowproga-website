Can a Brandon lawn be mowed after heavy rain? +

Only when the surface can support equipment without rutting; growth alone does not mean the ground is ready.

Should irrigation heads be marked? +

Yes. Raised heads beside turf edges can be damaged during careless mowing or trimming.

Are fallen palm fronds removed with a normal cut? +

Large debris collection should be requested as a separate cleanup item.

Does the grass slow down in Florida's cooler months? +

Warm-season turf generally reduces growth as temperatures drop, which can change how often it needs cutting.
