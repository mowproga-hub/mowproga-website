Riverview drainage questions

Will the crew drive a mower through standing water? +

No. Saturated ground can rut and may be unsafe for standard mowing equipment.

Does a drainage swale count in the lawn-size card? +

It should be identified separately so the operator can decide if ordinary mowing is suitable.

Why is grass high after a stormy week? +

Warm-season turf can grow rapidly with moisture and heat even when the ground remains soft.

What photos help with a drainage-area quote? +

Show the slope, wet spot and nearby firm lawn together from at least one side angle.
