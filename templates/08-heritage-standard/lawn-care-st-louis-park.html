Local questions

St. Louis Park shade questions

Will shaded grass need the same schedule as sunny turf? +

Not necessarily. Less light can slow growth and change how the lawn responds to cutting.

What should happen around a tree's exposed roots? +

A mower should stay clear; detailed trimming can address the border without scraping the roots.

Can autumn growth and leaf fall overlap? +

Yes. A lawn can still need cutting even while leaves are collecting across its surface.

Is a full leaf removal included with mowing? +

Heavy seasonal clearing is quoted separately from the usual maintenance pass.
