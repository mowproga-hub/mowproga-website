Should a Centennial lawn be cut especially short before hot weather? +

No. Leaving more leaf area can help protect turf when heat and wind increase moisture stress.

Are irrigation heads included in the trimming plan? +

Their locations need to be known so equipment can work around them without damage.

How are long rear fences handled? +

The mowing and string-trimming scope should account for the full fence-line length, not only the lawn area.
