Local questions

Answers for close-set Englewood lots

Will a mower fit between the sidewalk and my parking pad? +

A narrow strip may be better handled with compact equipment or trimming. Share the width rather than assuming a standard mower will fit.

Why is the boulevard grass browner than the backyard? +

Roadside sun and reflected heat can stress turf more than a sheltered section a few feet away.

Can access through the rear alley reduce yard disruption? +

It may be an option when the gate and surface are suitable; the entrance needs to be checked before planning.

Is a small Englewood yard always the least expensive? +

Not always. Several separated sections and many edges can require more finishing time than a single open lawn.
