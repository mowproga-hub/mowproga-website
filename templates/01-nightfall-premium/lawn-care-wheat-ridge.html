Local questions

Crown Hill-area care questions

Can mowing damage raised tree roots? +

A mower crossing exposed roots may scrape or strike them. A safer finishing plan stays clear of the root flare.

Do the planted beds receive lawn mowing service? +

Mowing ends at the agreed turf border; bed cleaning or shrub work is a separate service.

Why might shaded grass need less cutting? +

Lower sunlight can limit growth, particularly during a hot period when exposed turf behaves differently.

Should the first estimate include photos of edging? +

Yes, especially if brick, timber or stone borders create many short mower passes.
