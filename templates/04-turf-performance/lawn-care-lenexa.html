Local questions

Lenexa irrigation and lawn questions

Why do irrigation heads matter during edging? +

Raised heads near a walkway can be damaged by blades or string, so the operator needs to know their positions.

Should tree rings be cut right against the trunk? +

No. Bark and root flares need clearance from powered equipment.

Does fescue grow as quickly all summer? +

Usually not; a hot dry stretch may slow it substantially after vigorous spring growth.

What counts as cleanup after a visit? +

Blowing clippings off hard surfaces is routine; collecting a thick layer of leaves is different work.
