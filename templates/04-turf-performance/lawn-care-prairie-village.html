Local questions

Prairie Village tree-and-edge questions

Can a mower pass over exposed tree roots? +

Repeated contact can damage roots and equipment. A careful trim is more appropriate for those spots.

Does shade affect the cutting interval? +

It can, because less light often means slower growth than in the sunnier parts of the property.

Will flower beds be disturbed during edging? +

A defined turf boundary should be followed without cutting into the plantings.

Why might a small front yard take longer than expected? +

Several curved edges and root obstacles can require more hand finishing than a simple open lawn.
